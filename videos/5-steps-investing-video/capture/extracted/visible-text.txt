5 Steps to Start Investing — a beginner-friendly faceless explainer.

Hook: Want to start investing but don't know step one? Here are five moves to begin. From $0 to invested.

Step 1 — Set a clear goal. First, name your goal. Retirement, a house, freedom — your why sets the timeline. Goals: Retirement, Home, Freedom.

Step 2 — Build your safety net. Next, secure the base: an emergency fund and no high-interest debt. Invest from stability. Target 3 to 6 months of expenses. Pay off high-interest debt first.

Step 3 — Open the right account. Then open an account — a 401(k), IRA, or brokerage. The wrapper decides your tax break. Tax-advantaged accounts first.

Step 4 — Buy low-cost index funds. Now invest. Low-cost index funds spread your money across the whole market in one buy. Low fees, instant diversification.

Step 5 — Automate and stay consistent. Last, automate it. Invest a set amount monthly and let time do the heavy lifting. Auto-invest monthly. Time in market beats timing the market.

Recap: Goal, safety net, account, index funds, automate. Five steps — that's the whole start.

CTA: The best time to start was yesterday. The second best is today. Start today. Educational content — not financial advice.

Guardrails: Order matters — goal, then safety net, then account, then invest, then automate. Don't invest before the emergency fund and high-interest debt are handled.
