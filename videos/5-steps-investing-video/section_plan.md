# 5 Steps to Start Investing — Pin & Paper

## Film Direction

**Palette:** 60% warm paper canvas (native pin-and-paper grain, consistent every scene) / 30% hairline rules + cream pinned-card surfaces + chapter-label layering / 10% accent — brand-primary amber carries the active step numeral and the live focal element; brand-secondary green is the growth/seedling semantic (jar fill, tree, compounding curve) and never bleeds onto chrome; tertiary terracotta is the one "cut/cost" alarm color (debt thread, fee countdown). `--ink` green is print ink for body; if any surface goes pure white prefer the cream/`--paper-warm` step.
**Type:** display (Space Grotesk, tight, sentence case) = hero words and step titles; mono UPPERCASE wide tracking = eyebrows, "STEP 0X" lockups, source/disclaimer tags; Caveat hand-script = step numerals and marginal notes only, never body; body = terse card sentences.
**Motion:** entries `EASE.entry`; large type blocks and diagram panels heavy; pin/stamp reveals `EASE.emphasis` (settle within ±2° tilt, never counter-rotate to 0°); idle `EASE.drift` at the subtle floor (±2-3 px / ±1-2%, 2.5-4s cycle); exits none — scenes hold the final frame. Budget: ONE root-level macro camera move per scene (slow drift / push / dolly) + at most 1-2 secondary live elements (a breathing hero, one drifting pin); everything else rests.
**Ambient:** native warm paper texture + sparse paper-grain/fiber noise, full-bleed, identical scene to scene. Single medium — no mesh, swell, grid, scanline, or halftone layered on the paper. One persistent pinned seedling motif lives on the lower stage through the continue run (scenes 3→4→6).
**Never (film-wide):** no mesh gradients, no neon-on-black, no glow bloom, no purple-blue AI gradient, no floating bokeh, no slide/wipe/zoom inside a scene (the paper settles, it does not swipe), no body text set in the hand-script.
**Transitions:** crossfade + push-slide only at break seams (continue scenes carry no transition anchor).
**Stillness-before-climax:** scenes 5 and 8 only — the fee-hits-zero landing and the "start today" resolve.
**Register mix:** scene 1 kinetic typography (pinned title); scene 2 diagram (goal cards + timeline arrow); scenes 3–4 diagram (jar/debt, account folders) inside one continue run; scene 5 data-viz (coin→market grid + fee counter); scene 6 data-viz (calendar coin-drop + compounding curve); scene 7 diagram (vertical checklist); scene 8 kinetic typography (proverb + tree). No captured assets this film (`assetCandidates` all empty).
**Captions:** captions enabled — bottom ~17% band reserved film-wide; foreground anchors around y≈0.42×height; per-scene prose flags caption risk only where content pushes low.
